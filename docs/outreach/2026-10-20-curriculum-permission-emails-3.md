## 1. Kenya: to info@kicd.ac.ke

**Subject:** Permission request: CBC curriculum design learning outcomes in a free, open curriculum database

Dear Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development,

My name is Ian Wang. I maintain StandardGraph, a free and open-source database that lines up
school curriculum standards across countries. It holds about 175,000 standards from more than
300 curriculum systems and shows which learning outcome in one country matches an outcome in
another. Teachers and curriculum teams use it to compare systems and align materials.

I need to tell you up front that StandardGraph has included about 2,700 specific learning
outcomes from the Competency Based Curriculum designs. I added them before I began checking
reuse terms. The curriculum designs state that no part may be reproduced without KICD's prior
written permission, so I have taken them out of the next release and I am asking for that
permission now.

1. May StandardGraph include and redistribute the specific learning outcomes (the outcome text,
   grade and strand only, not the full curriculum designs) for Mathematics, Science, English and
   Social Studies?
2. If so, what credit would you like on each record?

If the answer is no, they stay out, and I will make sure they are removed from the copies
already published as well.

The project is free to use, released under the MIT license, and does not charge for access. If
you agree, Kenya's curriculum would be mapped to curricula in Uganda, Tanzania, Rwanda, South
Africa, the United Kingdom, the United States and others, and I am glad to share those mappings
with your team.

If another office handles these requests, I would be grateful if you could forward this.

Thank you for your time.

Ian Wang
StandardGraph
https://walkmakewalk.com/standardgraph.html
