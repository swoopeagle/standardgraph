## 1. Turkey: to ttkb@meb.gov.tr

**Subject:** Permission request: including the mathematics curriculum outcomes in a free, open standards database / Matematik dersi öğretim programı kazanımlarının açık bir veritabanında kullanımı için izin talebi

Dear Board of Education,

My name is Ian Wang. I maintain StandardGraph, a free and open-source database that lines up
school curriculum standards across countries. It holds about 175,000 standards from more than
300 curriculum systems, and it shows which outcome in one country matches an outcome in
another. Teachers and curriculum teams use it to compare systems and to align materials.

I would like to include the learning outcomes from the Türkiye Century Education Model
mathematics curricula (grades 1-4, 5-8 and 9-12, as published on mufredat.meb.gov.tr). I would
use the outcome codes and text only (for example MAT.5.1.1), not the full documents.

The curriculum pages carry the notice "MEB © Tüm Hakları Saklıdır", so I am asking before
using them. Specifically:

1. May I include the outcome codes and text in StandardGraph and redistribute them as part of
   the open dataset?
2. What attribution would you like? My default is "Source: T.C. Millî Eğitim Bakanlığı, Talim ve
   Terbiye Kurulu Başkanlığı" with a link to mufredat.meb.gov.tr on every record.

The project is free to use, under the MIT license, and does not charge for access. If you agree,
your curriculum would also be mapped to the curricula of the United States, the United Kingdom,
Singapore, France and others, and I am glad to share those mappings with your team.

If someone else at the Ministry handles these requests, I would be grateful if you could forward
this to them.

Thank you for your time.

Ian Wang
StandardGraph
https://walkmakewalk.com/standardgraph.html

## 2. Philippines: to bcd.od@deped.gov.ph

**Subject:** Request for approval: including MATATAG Mathematics competencies in a free, open curriculum standards database

Dear Director and the Bureau of Curriculum Development,

My name is Ian Wang. I maintain StandardGraph, a free and open-source database that lines up
curriculum standards across countries. It holds about 175,000 standards from more than 300
curriculum systems and shows which competency in one country matches a competency in another.
Teachers and curriculum teams use it to compare systems and align materials.

I would like to include the learning competencies from the MATATAG K to 10 Mathematics
Curriculum Guide. I would use the competency codes and text only, with DepEd credited on every
record and a link back to deped.gov.ph/curriculum-guides.

I understand that under Section 176 of RA 8293 there is no copyright in works of the government,
but that prior approval is needed for exploitation for profit. StandardGraph is free to use,
released under the MIT license, and does not charge for access. I would still rather ask than
assume. Could you:

1. Confirm that including and redistributing the competencies in this free, open dataset is
   acceptable, or grant approval if one is needed; and
2. Tell me the attribution you prefer?

If you agree, the MATATAG Mathematics competencies would be mapped to the curricula of the
United States, Singapore, the United Kingdom, France and others, and I am glad to share those
mappings with your team.

If another office handles these requests, I would be grateful if you could forward this.

Thank you for your time.

Ian Wang
StandardGraph
https://walkmakewalk.com/standardgraph.html
